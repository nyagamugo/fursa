04 / A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Fresh perspective.

Here’s what to know about this concept.

What is Fursa?+

Fursa is the name of this fresh-start marketing concept for your Clean Start hackathon initiative. The copy explores opportunity, connection, and small steps forward; it does not claim to describe verified app features.

How is it connected to Clean Start?+

You identified Fursa as part of your hackathon work with the Clean Start team. This page reflects that connection without attributing specific roles, outcomes, or features that haven’t been confirmed.

Can I use the app from this page?+

This is an interactive one-page preview, not a live service. You can explore the sections and try the fresh-start reflection below. No account is created and no information is sent.

What happens to my answers in the preview?+

Your selections stay on this page and reset when you reload. There’s no tracking, form submission, or storage of your answers.

How can I get involved?+

Start by exploring the idea and choosing a first step below. A verified contact or participation link can be added when the team’s public project details are available.
